# Walmart Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Walmart Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, revenue distribution, customer behavior, product category performance, payment preferences, and promotional effectiveness across multiple years (2023–2025). The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and dynamic filtering.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** 3,411.30K
- **Total Revenue:** 588.03K
- **Total Quantity:** 225.14K
- **Average Discount Rate:** 6.33%

## Dashboard Features
- **Sales by Region:** Compared sales performance across Central, East, South, and West regions.
- **Category-Wise Sales:** Analyzed sales distribution across product categories, including Electronics, Automotive, Health & Beauty, Office Supplies, and Sports & Outdoors.
- **Sales by Customer Segment:** Examined sales contributions from Consumer, Corporate, and Small Business customers.
- **Year & Quarterly Sales Analysis:** Visualized quarterly sales trends across 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- **Sales by Payment Method:** Compared customer payment preferences across Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Gift Card, and Walmart Pay.
- **Sales by Sales Channel:** Analyzed revenue distribution across In-Store, Online, and Pickup channels.
- **Sales by Promotion Period:** Compared sales performance between promotional and non-promotional periods.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Enabled dynamic filtering by year, region, and customer segment for focused analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## Key Learnings
- Developed practical skills in data cleaning, aggregation, and sales performance analysis.
- Practiced building interactive dashboards using Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers.
- Improved the ability to communicate business metrics and trends through data visualization.
- Gained experience in designing structured, user-friendly business intelligence dashboards.

## Project Objective
To transform sales data into an interactive reporting solution that helps users explore regional performance, product category trends, customer segments, payment methods, sales channels, and promotional sales patterns.
